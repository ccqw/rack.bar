## What's Working

1. **The live decode loop.** Scrimless keypad + accent "On the bar" line + bar resolving in real time collapses enter->verify into one glance. Best single design decision in the product, and a spec'd deliberate one.
2. **Divergence as a first-class object.** "0.5 KG SHORT" outline pill vs solid "OVER" pill vs green EXACT dot, with the reversible one-tap Round up / Use-under pair. Honesty is interaction design, not fine print.
3. **The truthful visualizer.** Real diameters, capacity that physically fills, engraved rotated numerals guaranteeing a non-color channel, tap-to-remove direct manipulation.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Palette layout shift after the first plate lands.** Adding the first plate swaps the hint for the taller ON THE BAR chip row, pushing the palette ~35px down mid-interaction; rapid taps hit the dead gap (empirically ate 2 of 3 taps). Chalk-hands commitment directly violated. Fix: reserve the chip row's height from the start. Related smaller instance: the round-up affordance appearing shifts the Target row. Suggested command: /impeccable layout
2. **[P1] No live regions, no headings.** Zero aria-live/role="status" and zero headings in the app; a screen-reader user typing a target hears nothing about the solved load or status. Per-control labeling is otherwise excellent, making this the one gap between the app and genuinely good SR support. Fix: role="status" on the statusline and keypad load line; visually-hidden section headings. Suggested command: /impeccable harden
3. **[P2] Sleeve-full is silent in By Plates.** The "Bar at capacity" pill exists in By Weight only; in By Plates the only signal is keys dimming to 30% opacity. Fix: surface the same capacity pill in By Plates. Suggested command: /impeccable harden
4. **[P2] Fullscreen copy bug: "61.23 kg - per side".** fullscreen.ts:214 appends "per side" to the other-unit TOTAL, factually wrong on the glance card. Fix: move the note to the config line (as the share card does). Suggested command: /impeccable clarify
5. **[P3] Below-bar target explains nothing.** Target 10 lb -> empty bar + "34 LB OVER". Fix: special-case copy: "Empty bar weighs 44 lb -- above your Target." Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

## Minor Observations

- Large discs bottom-clipped behind the keypad sheet at big loads -- the signature moment loses the discs' lower third.
- "315 lb EXACT" for 143 kg (true 315.26): display-rounding presented as exactness; should be a conscious stance.
- Recent chips re-render lossily across units; tap presumably restores exact kg but the label implies otherwise.
- Training set's single 45 lb bar renders as a full-width selected tile -- a "choice of one".
- One possibly-unlabeled button in rack-console (class `over`) in its empty state.
- The no-page-scroll composition holds only on tall phones; Share/recents fall below the fold on SE-class heights.
- Detector spec-coverage gaps worth folding into DESIGN.md frontmatter if intended: 46/52/58px display sizes, scrim/overlay rgba values, sleeve geometry radii.

## Questions to Consider

1. Does Decode need "Done" at all? The solve is live; could closing be implicit (idle commit) without violating the no-outside-tap rule?
2. Where does domain honesty end and cross-unit confusion begin? In lb display on the Competition set the palette says "25" (kg) while a tap adds 110 lb to the total.
3. Is fullscreen showing the right thing? It exists for across-the-rack glancing but shows totals while burying the per-side plate list a loading partner acts on.
